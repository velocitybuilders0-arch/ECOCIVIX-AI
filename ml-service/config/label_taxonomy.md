# ECOCIVIX AI — Locked Priority Label Taxonomy

This document defines the official, locked priority classification taxonomy for citizen reports in ECOCIVIX AI.

## Priority Levels

- **LOW**: cosmetic / non-urgent; no health, safety, or environmental harm; no service disruption.
- **MEDIUM**: real but non-imminent disruption to services or environment; scheduled action required.
- **HIGH**: significant risk to health, safety, or environment; action required within days.
- **CRITICAL**: imminent danger to life, or active large-scale environmental hazard; immediate escalation.

## Annotator Tie-Break Rule

When ambiguous, choose the HIGHER label only if the text itself evidences imminent harm; otherwise default to the LOWER label.
